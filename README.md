# Summer of Science 2026

Welcome to the **Summer of Science 2026** repository! This is a self-learning program where participants explore topics in mathematics, physics, computer science, and related fields over the summer.

## About the Program

Summer of Science (SoS) is a mentorship-driven initiative that encourages students to dive deep into a subject of their choice through independent study. Participants are paired with mentors who guide them throughout the summer.

## How It Works

1. **Apply** – Participants submit their project proposals outlining the topic they wish to study.
2. **Get Matched** – Each participant is matched with a suitable mentor.
3. **Study** – Participants work through reading material, exercises, and projects with periodic check-ins.
4. **Report** – A final report or presentation summarises what was learned over the summer.

## Projects

Projects from this cohort will be listed here as they are registered. Each project folder contains:
- A project description
- Reading list / resources
- Notes and progress updates
- Final report
