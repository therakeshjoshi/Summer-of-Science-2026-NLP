# Summer-of-Science-2026

## Tentative Roadmap

- **Week 1:** Introduction to NLP & Text Preprocessing & Simple NLP Pipeline  
- **Week 2:** Text Representation: From BoW to TF-IDF  
- **Week 3:** Word Embeddings 
- **Week 4:** Neural Networks and PyTorch  
  - **Midterm Submission**  
- **Week 5:** Recurrent Neural Networks & Sequence Modeling  
- **Week 6:** Attention Mechanism & Seq2Seq Models  
- **Week 7:** The Transformer Architecture & Pretrained Language Models: BERT & GPT  
- **Week 8:** Final Project

## Summary

This roadmap covers the complete progression of modern Natural Language Processing, beginning with text preprocessing and representation methods, moving through word embeddings and neural networks, advancing to sequence modeling and attention mechanisms, and finally exploring Transformers and pretrained language models such as BERT and GPT. The program concludes with a final project integrating the concepts learned
